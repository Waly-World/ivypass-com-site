How it works

  1. Scan. A tag on the product, a code on the seat, a card in the hand.
  2. Have it. The passport exists the moment the page opens. Nothing to download, nothing to fill in.
  3. Come back. Every later scan, at a store, an event or at home, adds to the same passport. Save it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and it stays.

Digital product passports

The EU is introducing digital passports for products sold in Europe, with textiles among the first groups. IVYPASS makes that passport something a customer wants to open: the product’s origin and care, and the brand’s next invitation, behind the same scan.
