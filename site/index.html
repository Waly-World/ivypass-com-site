Three ways in. One passport.

  • I own it. A tag sewn into the jersey.
  • I saw it. A code in the video.
  • I was there. The screen in the stadium.

The first scan creates the passport. Every new touchpoint adds its card to the same one. Scan one you already hold and you are straight back inside. Hold the set, and it can open something none of them opens alone: a discount, a gift, a door.

At Oxford United, one matchday had four cards to collect, from the club’s email to the club shop. Fans who held all four could enter the competition.

How it works

  1. Scan. A tag on the product, a code on the stadium seat, a card in the hand.
  2. Have it. The passport exists the moment the page opens. Nothing to download, nothing to fill in.
  3. Come back. Every later scan, at a store, an event or at home, adds to the same passport. Save it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and it stays.

Digital product passports

The EU is introducing digital passports for products sold in Europe, with textiles among the first groups. IVYPASS makes that passport something a customer wants to open: the product’s origin and care, and the brand’s next invitation, behind the same scan.
